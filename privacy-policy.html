Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Last updated: November 2026

The short version: PromptByEye has no server of its own. We do not receive, store, or have access to your username, your API key, your images, your prompts, or your history — everything in this policy is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This policy explains exactly what stays on your phone, and the one place information does go: Google Gemini, when you choose to use a tool.

Who we are

PromptByEye is published by AB CODERS. For privacy questions, contact us through the support email shown on this app's Google Play listing.

1. What is stored on your phone

Only on this device, and nowhere else:

  • Your username (the name you pick when you set up the app).
  • Your Gemini and Groq API keys, if you add them (up to 5 of each). They are kept in the app's encrypted storage, protected by your phone's own security (Android Keystore). The app rotates between your Gemini keys automatically when one reaches its daily limit.
  • Your voice, only while you speak to Aira: recordings are sent to the service you pick in Settings (Google Gemini, or Groq, or your phone's own voice typing) only to turn speech into text. Recordings are never stored — not by us, not on your phone. Only the text is kept, inside your history, like anything Aira creates.
  • Your saved results and history: the prompts and answers the tools created, titles, the tool used, the time, and your favourites.
  • Reports you make about a result you did not like (the reason you picked and a short excerpt), kept only on this device.
  • Your app settings, such as which AI model you picked, whether you set an app PIN, and the date you accepted these terms.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We run no server, no account system, and no database outside your phone.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We never see your username, your key, your photos, your text, or your results. We could not show them to anyone even if asked — we simply do not have them.

3. What goes to Google Gemini

The tools in this app work by sending your request to Google Gemini, Google's AI service, using your own Gemini API key. When you use a tool, the text you typed — and the photo, if you picked one for an image tool — is sent from your phone directly to Google. Google handles it under Google's own terms and privacy policy, and your use of your API key is governed by your agreement with Google. PromptByEye is not involved in that transfer and keeps no copy outside your phone.

Important — free vs paid keys: if your API key uses Google's free (unpaid) tier, Google may use the content you submit and the responses generated to improve its products, and human reviewers at Google may read that content. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information with a free-tier key. If your key is on a paid plan, Google states it does not use your prompts or responses to improve its products.

Either way: do not put highly sensitive personal information (passwords, bank details, identity documents) into any AI tool, in this app or anywhere else.

Personalisation: to make answers personal, a short summary of your activity in this app is included in requests sent to Google Gemini with your own key. It never goes anywhere else. You can see the exact summary, edit it, clear it, or turn personalisation off anytime in Settings → My data.

4. Deleting your information

Because everything lives only on this phone:

  • Deleting the app deletes everything with it.
  • Clearing the app's data in your phone's settings deletes everything.
  • In Settings you can clear your history, clear all data, or choose "Delete my account", which immediately and permanently erases your profile, your key, and all saved results from this device.

There is no backup and no copy anywhere else, so deleted information cannot be recovered — by you or by us. See also our account deletion page.

5. Age

PromptByEye is for users aged 18 and over only. It is not designed for children.

6. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy inside the app will be updated and given a new version and date, and you will be asked to accept it again before continuing.

Questions: because the app has no server and collects nothing, most questions answer themselves — your information is on your phone, under your control. For anything else, contact us through the support email shown on this app's Google Play listing.
