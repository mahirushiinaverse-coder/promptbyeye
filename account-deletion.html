Deleting Your Account

Version 1 · Last updated: November 2026

Delete your account in the app:

  1. Open PromptByEye and go to Settings.
  2. Choose "Delete my account".
  3. Confirm. Your account and everything with it is erased from your phone immediately and permanently.

How deletion works in PromptByEye

PromptByEye accounts exist only on your phone — there is no server copy. Your "account" is a local profile: the username you chose during setup. It is not registered with us, and we hold no copy of it or of anything else in the app.

Because of that, deletion happens on your device, takes effect at once, and cannot be undone. There is nothing stored anywhere else for us to delete afterwards.

What gets deleted

"Delete my account" immediately and permanently erases, from this device:

  • your profile and username,
  • your Gemini and Groq API keys as stored in the app,
  • all saved results and history,
  • all reports and settings.

After deletion, the app returns to the sign-up screen, and you can create a fresh local account if you wish. If a result matters to you, copy it, share it, or use "Export my history" in Settings before deleting — deleted information cannot be recovered, by you or by us.

If you no longer have the app

You do not need to contact us to delete your data. Any of these does the same job, completely:

  • Reinstall PromptByEye, then use Settings → "Delete my account" as above.
  • In your phone's Settings, open Apps → PromptByEye → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstall the app — deleting the app deletes everything stored in it.

What we hold

Nothing. PromptByEye is published by AB CODERS and runs without a server: we never receive your username, your keys, your photos, your prompts, or your history, so there is no copy on our side to delete, keep, or hand over. When you use a tool, your request goes from your phone directly to Google Gemini with your own API key, under Google's terms — see the Privacy Policy for the full picture, including the note about free vs paid keys.

Still need help?

If you have a question about deletion, or you still want to ask us to look into something, contact us through the support email shown on PromptByEye's Google Play listing. Please understand that because your data never leaves your phone to us, the deletion itself can only be completed on your device, using the steps above.
